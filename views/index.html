      Severe Bleeding

      • Direct Pressure: Apply firm, continuous direct pressure with sterile cloth/gauze without lifting.
      • Elevate: Keep injured limb raised above heart level if no fracture is suspected.
      • Do Not Remove: If dressing soaks through, add more layers directly on top.
      🧪

      Chemical Spill / Acid

      • Wash With Water: Flush affected skin/eyes with copious running water for at least 15–20 minutes.
      • Remove Clothing: Carefully remove contaminated clothing without spreading chemical.
      • Do Not Neutralize: Never apply neutralizing chemicals without HAZMAT direction.
      🔥

      Thermal Burns

      • Cool Water: Cool the burn immediately under cool, gentle running water for 10–20 minutes.
      • No Ice or Butter: NEVER use ice, ice water, oil, or greasy ointments.
      • Cover Loosely: Protect with a clean, dry, non-adherent sterile dressing.
      ☀️

      Heatstroke / Fainting

      • Cool Shade: Move victim to a cool, shaded or air-conditioned area immediately.
      • Elevate Feet: Loosen tight clothing; elevate legs/feet 12 inches if fainting.
      • Hydrate Carefully: Provide small sips of cool water ONLY if fully conscious and responsive.
